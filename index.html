Product Details

The Genuine Leather Money Bag is a long wallet designed to hold everything you carry every day — notes, coins, cards, receipts and even your phone — in one organised place. The soft leather and neat stitching give it a premium look that also makes it a great gift.

What problem does it solve?

Small wallets force you to fold notes and leave cards loose in your pocket. This long money bag keeps banknotes flat and unfolded, holds all your cards in dedicated slots, and gives you a zipped section for coins — so nothing falls out and everything is easy to find.

Who is it for?

  • Anyone who carries a lot of cash and cards daily
  • Business owners, shopkeepers and collectors
  • Travellers who want passport, tickets and cash in one place
  • A practical gift for family or friends

How it's organised

Open the wallet to find a full-length cash compartment for flat notes, multiple card slots, a clear ID window, a zipped coin pocket and a separate sleeve that fits most smartphones. The button/zip closure keeps everything secure.

Care & usage

  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth to keep the leather clean.
  • Avoid soaking it in water or leaving it in direct heat.
  • Do not overstuff the card slots to keep the shape neat.
  • Use a little leather conditioner occasionally for long life.
